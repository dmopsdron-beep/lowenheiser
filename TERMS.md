## Safety Notice

This application displays live telemetry from a generator and flight controller system, including engine RPM, battery state, and alarm conditions. It is a **monitoring aid**, not a safety-certified instrument. Always:

- Verify critical readings (voltage, RPM, temperatures) against the vehicle's own instrumentation before making operational decisions
- Follow your generator and autopilot manufacturer's safety procedures
- Do not rely solely on this software to detect equipment failure
